# Learning interface

The interface keeps the warm paper, Chinese typography and vermilion action
identity. Depth comes from edges, contact shadows and interaction, rather than
giving every surface the same red gradient.

## Attention and content

- The course chooser is a compact library with a distinct source cue for each
  course. Course names and descriptions appear once.
- Home emphasizes the actual next class. Trail and due-review counts remain
  available without dominating the lesson path.
- Navigation uses four stable destinations and a single moving selection
  indicator. It must preserve safe-area padding, keyboard focus and overlay
  isolation.
- Teaching presents one insight at a time. Context, cues and additional source
  examples are explicitly revealed. Extra examples stay in the current unit.
- Dialogue foregrounds the current exchange and the learner's reply. Earlier
  turns and longer context are secondary disclosures.
- Grammar review teaches sentence parts and differences individually. Whole
  patterns and raw comparisons remain available on demand.
- Books use a quieter reading lane. Source passages stay in order and are
  revealed at the reader's pace. Each chapter adds an explanation, a labeled
  practice scenario, a belief reflection and a saved action to revisit.

## Motion

Entry and reveal motion is finite. Pointer depth and navigation movement use
bounded springs, stop when settled and cancel on unmount. Speech highlighting
tracks the actual utterance. Reduced-motion preference changes cancel ongoing
motion and retain clear static states. Motion must never delay a usable control.

## Learning integrity

The English recall cue defines the exercise and stays visible even when reading
translations are hidden. Expected Hanzi remains hidden before assessment unless
the learner explicitly reveals assistance. Neither animation nor completing a
screen awards mastery; unaided retrieval evidence across sessions does.

Book comparison does not verify arbitrary alternative grammar. Source-grounded
explanations are separate from the optional grammar assessment service. Drawing
classification ranks the independent corpus before comparing the expected word.

Book scenarios illustrate decisions rather than reporting invented business
results. A written reflection is a learner's own explanation, not an automatic
understanding verdict. Reading, source checks, planned actions and actions tried
must remain distinguishable. Journal drafts live separately from chapter
completion and existing vocabulary progress.
