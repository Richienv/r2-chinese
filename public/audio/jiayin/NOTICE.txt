Jiayin interview practice audio

These files are generated Mandarin speech, not recordings supplied by Jiayin,
the PDF author, an interviewer, or the HSK textbook publisher.
The spoken text is taken verbatim from src/data/jiayin.json: its vocabulary,
sentence chunks and complete answers. Indonesian explanations are not spoken.

Generator: scripts/generate-interview-audio.py
Engine: Piper (https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl)
Voice: zh_CN-huayan-medium, 22,050 Hz source, encoded as mono 24 kHz MP3.
Model and model card:
https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/zh/zh_CN/huayan/medium
The model repository lists the MIT license. The voice's model card separately
describes its training dataset license as unknown. No training dataset or
model weights are distributed with this app.

src/data/jiayin-audio.json records the model checksum, exact text mapping,
measured clip durations and individual MP3 checksums. Word-boundary timing is
not inferred: these clips use line-level playback.
